About CapacityConnect

A business-oriented learning management prototype for SIH26075.

Platform purpose

CapacityConnect brings learning, assessments, progress visibility and learning-gap identification into one structured workspace for trainees, trainers and administrators.

✓Centralized course and learning access
✓Assessment and performance tracking
✓Topic-level learning gap indication
✓Clear dashboard for learning operations
